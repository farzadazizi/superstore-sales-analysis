
# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
An end-to-end Business Intelligence project analyzing retail sales and profitability using Databricks SQL.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze sales and profit performance.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing product categories.
- Evaluate regional business performance.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Identify the most valuable and loss-making customers.
- Explore monthly and yearly sales trends.

## Tools & Technologies
- Databricks
- SQL
- Git & GitHub
- Databricks SQL Dashboards

## Key Business Metrics

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $2,297,200.86 |
| Total Profit | $286,397.02 |
| Profit Margin | 12.47% |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |

## Key Findings
- Technology generated the highest category profit.
- Furniture had a significantly lower profit margin than other categories.
- The West region generated the highest total profit.
- Higher discount ranges were associated with lower profitability.
- November recorded the highest aggregated monthly sales.
